Overview

Change as a permanent condition

Most organisations still treat modernisation as a programme with a start date, a finish line and a temporary surge of attention. When the programme ends, the underlying pace of change does not. MbDD argues for a different operating model: one that keeps improving as part of everyday work, and that still knows when to stop and redesign deliberately.

01

Modernisation by Default

The continuous, often quiet work of keeping systems, teams and practices current. It is the habit of reducing friction, retiring debt and improving how work gets done without waiting for a flagship initiative. Done well, it becomes the organisation’s normal rhythm rather than an exception.

02

Modernisation by Design

The intentional moments when direction must change: a new architecture, a different operating model, a deliberate break with how things have been done. Design is not constant upheaval. It is the capacity to decide, redesign and commit when the default path is no longer enough.

Who it is for

People responsible for keeping change workable

The book is written for people who already carry the weight of technology decisions in complex organisations, not for readers looking for slogans or quick fixes.

  • Technology leaders Chief technology officers, digital directors and senior engineering leaders who need modernisation to survive beyond a single programme cycle.
  • Architects Enterprise and solutions architects who translate strategy into systems that must remain changeable under real constraints.
  • Delivery and transformation teams Programme, product and transformation practitioners who want continuous improvement and deliberate redesign to reinforce each other rather than compete.
